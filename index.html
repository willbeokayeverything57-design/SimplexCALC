Step-by-Step Simplex Method

A short guide to the usual maximization tableau procedure.

1

Convert to standard form

Write the objective function and constraints in standard form. For ≤ constraints, add slack variables.

2

Choose the pivot column

In the common maximization tableau convention, choose the most negative coefficient in the bottom row.

3

Choose the pivot row

Divide each eligible positive pivot-column entry into its right-hand-side value. Choose the smallest non-negative ratio.

4

Perform pivot operations

Make the pivot value 1, then make the other values in that column 0.

5

Check the result

Repeat until there are no negative coefficients left in the bottom row. Then read the solution.
